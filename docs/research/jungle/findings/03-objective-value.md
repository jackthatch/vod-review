# Axis 3 — Objective value and prioritisation

> Compiled from the first research run (2026-09-24); findings salvaged from
> sub-agent summary output, sourced primarily to **Riot primary documents**
> (patch notes and `/dev` blogs). **Highest-quality axis of the three** — real
> Riot-published numbers. Still worth a spot-verify.

## Riot published (partial) win-rate-after-objective numbers

Riot's "/dev: How the Season 1 Changes Landed" (2025-02-07) gives a "**Percent Win
Rate after Claiming Each Objective**" table — but only for **First Blood** and
**First Turret**: First Blood went **57.6% (14.24) → 55.4% (25.S1.2)**; First
Turret **70.4% → 70.3%**, concluding "game pacing and overall snowballing are in
roughly the same place"
[Riot /dev](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-how-the-season-1-changes-landed/)
(accessed 2026-09-24, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary).
**Drakes, grubs, Herald, Baron and Atakhan were not in the table.**

## Atakhan existed briefly, then was removed

Atakhan (2025 season) spawned at **20:00** in the top or bottom river, its form
(**Ruinous** = action-heavy; **Voracious** = low-action) and lane determined by
combat by 14:00. Riot *targeted* 50–70% Ruinous, but live data came in at **85%
Ruinous / 15% Voracious** and **44% top / 56% bottom**, and Riot conceded **"both
have been a bit too strong"**
[Riot /dev: 2025 Season One Gameplay Preview](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-2025-season-one-gameplay-preview/)
(confidence: High, tier: Primary).

**Atakhan was then removed in patch 26.1 (2026)**, along with Blood Roses and
Feats of Strength. Riot's stated reason: Atakhan "contributed to an extremely
objective-focused game that tended to narrow the possibilities of what a game of
Summoner's Rift can be"
[Riot Patch 26.1 Notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/)
(accessed 2026-09-24, confidence: High, tier: Primary).

## Current state (patch 26.x, 2026) — the 2026 direction

Per the 2026 Season One preview and patch 26.1: **Baron returns to a 20:00 spawn**;
Feats of Strength removed and **first-blood (100g) / first-turret (300g) gold
restored**; Riot explicitly wants to swing away from a "teamfighting over neutral
objectives" meta toward splitting/sieging. Riot also notes that **"some roles like
jungle and support are much more influential"** and promises balance changes to
increase role agency
[Riot /dev: 2026 Season One Gameplay Preview](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-2026-season-one-gameplay-preview/)
(confidence: High, tier: Primary).

## Voidgrubs

Patch 25.09 (2025-04-29): spawn moved **6:00 → 8:00**, **no longer respawns**,
Hunger of the Void now at **3 grubs (from 4)**, Feats credit at 2/3 grubs
[Riot Patch 25.09 Notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-25-09-notes/)
(confidence: High, tier: Primary). Riot frames grubs/Herald as **tempo/structural**
objectives, while strengthening **dragon/Baron comeback XP** — i.e. drakes and
Baron are treated as comeback levers (confidence: Medium, Primary).

## Dragon Soul ≈ Baron (design intent, not measurement)

Patch 26.1: **"Claiming Dragon Soul is just as impactful as taking Baron Nashor,
so we want the time commitment for doing so to be comparable"** — and Riot made
epic monsters **10–30% longer to kill** (late drakes slower still) while buffing
Smite [Riot Patch 26.1 Notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/)
(confidence: Medium, tier: Primary). This is **stated design intent**, not a
measured win-rate equivalence.

Riot's Win-Probability model lists objectives (dragon kills incl. soul, Herald
trinket, Baron timers, Elder) among its predictive features — evidence Riot treats
them as outcome drivers, though no per-objective coefficients are published
[lolesports Dev Diary](https://lolesports.com/article/dev-diary-win-probability-powered-by-aws-at-worlds/blt0)
(confidence: Medium, tier: Primary).

> Conflicts noted: Riot's Atakhan form-distribution target (50–70% Ruinous) was
> contradicted by live data (85/15) — Riot flagged the miss itself. Riot's
> "Soul ≈ Baron" claim is design intent, not a measurement.
> Gaps: **No sources found** for measured win rates of individual drakes, Dragon
> Soul, Voidgrubs, Rift Herald, Baron, or Atakhan (all stat sites blocked); no
> first-drake correlation figure; grubs-vs-first-drake expected value unquantified.

## Why this matters for the coach

Riot's 2026 direction (less teamfight-over-objective, more splitting/sieging)
should shape the coach's objective advice: **don't assume every objective must be
contested.** That aligns with VISION's "concede and trade is often correct"
principle — now with a Riot-stated design rationale behind it.
